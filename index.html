Ask us something else ↗
How do I start a project?＋

Start with a short introduction to your idea. Include the project type, location, approximate size, and what you would like to achieve. These details help shape an initial design brief.

What should I prepare for a discussion?＋

Bring any available site information, measurements, photographs, inspiration images, and a list of spaces you need. It is also useful to share your target budget and timeline.

Can I discuss an idea that is not fully developed?＋

Yes. Your initial idea can be a starting point for discussing priorities, exploring possibilities, and defining what the project needs to do.

How are scope, fees, and timelines decided?＋

These depend on the project brief and the services required. Confirm the deliverables, schedule, and fees in a project-specific proposal before work begins.
